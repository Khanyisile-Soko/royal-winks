Our Policies

To ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for every Royal Winks client, please take note of our booking policies.

01

If any changes need to be made to your appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to avoid additional charges.

02

No kids or extra guests are allowed.

03

Arriving late will cut into your appointment time and may result in additional charges. If your appointment has to be cancelled, the applicable cancellation terms will apply.

04

Appointments that are one hour late will incur an additional fee of R30.

05

A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking. The deposit will be deducted from your total bill. The deposit is non-refundable if the appointment is missed.

06

Please arrive without any eye makeup and do not curl your lashes before your appointment.

07

If your lashes become dislodged or you choose to remove them after 36 hours, a complimentary service will not be provided.
